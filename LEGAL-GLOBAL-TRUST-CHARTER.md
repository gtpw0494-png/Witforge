# THE WITFORGE CHARTER
## Global Trust & Compliance Covenant

**Version** 1.0.0 · **Effective** 2026-09-19 · **Status** RECORD — not legal advice
**Instrument class** Constitutional — this Charter ranks above every other WitForge
policy; where any other policy is silent or conflicts, this Charter governs.

---

### Preamble

WitForge is a decentralized software platform operated for a global public. It is
incorporated under no flag, owes allegiance to no border, and draws its legitimacy
from a single source: the covenant it makes, identically, with every person who
uses it everywhere on Earth.

A platform that claims no home jurisdiction cannot claim the protection of any
legal system either. It therefore binds itself — voluntarily, publicly, and in
advance — to the only obligation that can follow a user into every country on
earth: **to be correct, to be honest, and to be trustworthy.**

That obligation is the whole of WitForge's law to its users. Everything else in
this document is machinery for keeping it.

---

### Article I — Nature of the Platform

1. **Decentralized by design.** WitForge is local-first: each deployment holds
   its own data, its own ledger, and its own authority. No central operator is
   required for a user's instance to function, and no central operator may act
   inside a user's instance uninvited.
2. **Global and uniform.** This Charter applies to every user in every country,
   on identical terms. No user receives a lesser covenant because of where they
   live.
3. **Self-governing, not ungoverned.** The absence of allegiance to any state is
   not the absence of law. The law of this Platform is this Charter, the
   policies registered beneath it, and the technical instruments that enforce
   them.

### Article II — The Prime Covenant

The Platform owes every user three duties, without exception, expiry, or
geographic limit:

1. **Correctness.** What the Platform reports as done must actually be done.
   An action is reported only with evidence of its execution; a state is
   reported only as measured. Simulation is always labelled simulation.
2. **Honesty.** What the Platform cannot do, it says it cannot do. Unavailable
   connectors report UNAVAILABLE with the path to make them available. No
   capability, integration, balance, or result is ever faked, implied, or
   staged.
3. **Trustworthiness.** The Platform is predictable before it is impressive.
   Authority is requested, scoped, recorded, and revocable. High-risk action is
   approval-gated. The user's "no" outranks every agent, every automation, and
   every convenience.

A breach of the Prime Covenant by any component is treated as a security
incident under the Incident Response Policy — not as a product defect.

### Article III — Self-Binding Obligations to Users

The Platform binds itself, enforceably against itself, to the following:

1. **Auditable truth.** Every consequential action is written to an audit
   record the user can read. Claims the user cannot inspect are not made.
2. **Data sovereignty.** User data lives in the user's deployment. There is no
   telemetry out, no profile built, no third party silently served. Export is
   available; deletion is real (WIPE-confirmed reset, per-record deletes).
3. **Informed consent.** The user's request grants permission — never the
   reverse. Permissions carry scope, level, and expiry. Confirmation phrases
   are single-use and are never themselves a grant.
4. **Honest money.** Balances, prices, and rewards are labelled for what they
   are: simulation until real-money obligations are satisfied. No real-money
   flow activates without the compliance gates in the Financial Risk Policy
   being met and the user's explicit confirmation recorded.
5. **Accountable automation.** Agents propose; the user disposes. An agent may
   never widen its own authority, mark its own work verified, or pretend a
   human approved what no human approved.
6. **Security first.** Security services surround every connector and every
   model. There is no privileged bypass — not for the Platform's own agents.

### Article IV — Standing Toward External Law

1. **No allegiance.** The Platform is bound by no single state's law as a matter
   of allegiance, loyalty, or incorporation. Its obligations run to its users,
   not to flags.
2. **Fact, not fealty.** Users and operators live in real jurisdictions, and
   mandatory local law may apply to *them* as a matter of physical fact. Where
   such law compels, this Charter yields to the minimum extent required — and
   the Platform must say so plainly, in the open, in the audit record. The
   Prime Covenant itself is never yielded: no external rule may require the
   Platform to lie to a user.
3. **Self-imposed gates.** Lest "bound by none" become licence to harm, the
   Platform locks its own most dangerous capabilities (real-money economy,
   wagering, bulk outreach) behind compliance gates it may not open for
   itself. These gates open only on documented legal sufficiency plus explicit
   owner confirmation.
4. **No weaponisation of decentralization.** Decentralization is a privacy and
   resilience property. It is not a tool for evading the user-facing
   obligations of this Charter, and reading it that way is a breach.

### Article V — Rights of the User

Every user, by virtue of using the Platform, holds:

1. The right to **inspect** the audit record of what was done in their name.
2. The right to **export** everything the Platform holds of theirs, in the
   open, without charge or friction.
3. The right to **delete** — really delete — with a confirmation the Platform
   honours completely.
4. The right to **refuse** any capability, any connector, any agent — and to
   have refusal cost them nothing but the declined feature.
5. The right to a **human path**: captchas, consents, and identity checks are
   routed to the human, never impersonated.
6. The right to **complain** and have the complaint owner-reviewed and
   recorded, under the Complaints Policy.

### Article VI — Enforcement

This Charter is enforced by instrument, not by promise:

- **The audit trail** records consequential action with actor and time.
- **The evidence vault** binds verification claims to tamper-evident proof.
- **Self-tests and release gates** (build, lint, six test suites, §126
  self-test) block a release whose behaviour contradicts its claims.
- **Truth-states** (LIVE / PARTIAL / EXTERNAL / LOCKED / POLICY) publish the
  real coverage of every requirement — including this Charter's own.
- **Incident Response** treats any breach of the Prime Covenant as: LOCKDOWN →
  contain → evidence → recover → report.

### Article VII — Amendment

1. This Charter and all policies beneath it are **versioned and additive**. No
   amendment rewrites or deletes history; a new version appends with a new
   effective date.
2. No amendment may reduce a user right, weaken a truth obligation, or apply
   retroactively to the user's detriment.
3. The effective version in force is the version recorded in the Platform's
   legal register — never a promise about some other version.

### Article VIII — Interpretation

- **Severability.** If any provision of this Charter fails in some place or
  time, the remainder stands — and the Prime Covenant stands first and last.
- **Language.** The English text is canonical. Translations are courtesies;
  where they drift, the canonical text and the Prime Covenant govern.
- **Supremacy.** This Charter sits above the Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy,
  the Acceptable Use Policy, the Economic Terms, and every other registered
  policy. They interpret it; they never trump it.
